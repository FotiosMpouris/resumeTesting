Building the harness.

The Point

The models will keep getting better. What matters is the harness — the infrastructure that makes them useful. Think of it like this: a language model is a brain, but a brain without hands, eyes, or a voice can’t do anything for you. The harness is the agents, voice pipelines, memory systems, and APIs that give AI the ability to actually operate inside your business. We’re model-agnostic — the best model for the job gets the job. Everything below is running in production right now.
