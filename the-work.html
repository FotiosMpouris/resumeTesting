LIVE

A guided lead system with photo analysis

A website and lead system for an exterior remodeling company. Instead of a sales push, homeowners go through a short guided walkthrough at their own pace, with a plain reason for each question. They can add photos of their home, and an OpenAI vision model looks at the siding, trim, gutters and any visible damage. With no photos, it reviews Street View of the address instead. Property research runs on every lead, so the contractor starts the first call with a full brief.

Guided walkthrough · photo or Street View analysis · property research · one-click import into my CRM

MVP LIVE

An AI-native CRM for home services

My AI-native CRM for home services and exterior contracting, MVP live with a launch customer. It follows a job from lead to cash: pipeline, an AI job brief that marks unknowns as TBD instead of guessing, pricing and versioned proposals, change orders, materials, invoicing and closeout. Receipts are checked against what was actually ordered, and there is a QuickBooks handoff. It’s a working build with real invoices going through it, not a prototype.

Row-level security · agent API and MCP server with scoped, audit-logged access · tests and CI

Custom AI agents

I build and run my own AI agents and use them every day. Ethos, my always-on agent, runs on its own server (first on OpenClaw, now on Hermes Agent) and takes work over Telegram by voice or text.
