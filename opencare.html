The Problem

53 million unpaid family caregivers in the US are coordinating doctors, insurance, medications, and siblings through group texts and spreadsheets. I was one of them. So I built the tool I needed.

OpenCare

Create a Care Circle — a shared workspace for everyone involved in someone’s care. Assign tasks across 19 categories, set recurring schedules, keep everyone informed through automated email and Telegram digests. Even family members who barely check email get reached.

Care Circles

Private workspaces for family and care teams. Add members by email.

Tasks & Reminders

19 categories. Recurring schedules. Daily digests.

No App Required

Offline contacts get email with full task details. Zero friction.

ID Verification

Government ID check through Persona. Bilingual. Installable as a PWA.

Recover E

An AI recovery companion living inside OpenCare’s recovery circles. 10 modalities, daily check-ins, its own memory, its own personality. Sharp, challenging, built for people who want to do the work. Voice-enabled.

This isn’t a chatbot menu. It reads and writes real recovery data, remembers your history, and holds you accountable.

The Poor People App

OpenCare is the first product under the PPA umbrella — a platform for the rest of us. The goal: let AI handle the 95% of coordination that’s purely procedural, so humans can focus on safety and real judgment.

The full vision — decentralized identity on Nostr, community matching, Sentinel trust network, contribution ledger — is documented at poorpeople.app. The infrastructure is being built to learn, integrate, and serve families in every community.
